We can use the same policy in two different roles: As the student for the initial attempt
and as the teacher to determine the value of actions in hindsight.
We introduce Self-Distillation Policy Optimization (SDPO) which repeatedly distills
the self-teacher into the student. Given a question x, we first sample rollouts from the
student πθ and obtain corresponding environment feedback. We then use the KL-divergence,
KL(p∥q) = ∑i p(i) log p(i)/q(i), as a distance measure for the next-token distributions of
student and teacher, and optimize a standard logit distillation loss:
